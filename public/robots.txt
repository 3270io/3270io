# The landing page is public in full and there is nothing to hold back. The file
# exists so crawlers get a 200 and a sitemap pointer rather than a 404 from the
# SPA fallback, which some of them treat as "no rules could be fetched" and back
# off from.
#
# The documentation sites are separate origins (3270web.3270.io and
# 3270connect.3270.io) and carry their own robots.txt — a rule written here
# would not apply to them. They publish their own sitemaps too; a sitemap may
# only list URLs under its own host, so ours does not repeat theirs.
#
# The sitemap holds one URL, because the site is one page: the product sections
# are anchors on the landing page, not routes a crawler can fetch on its own.
# Keep the notes here rather than in sitemap.xml — that file must contain
# nothing but the urlset, and its <lastmod> wants bumping when the page changes.

User-agent: *
Allow: /
